# Project screenshot provenance

These seven images are authentic captures of the project interfaces. They were sourced from their project screenshot and verification collections, then proportionally resized as needed and encoded as WebP at quality 90. No screenshot was invented, cropped, or edited to alter its interface.

- **GitSomething**: interactive demo workspace captures, including the orbit repository and demonstration identities.
- **FunClipper**: captures of the running application using controlled test-game entries and generated color-bar test footage. Displayed PC vitals are sampled test-machine readings, not performance benchmarks.
- **MonsterGarage**: workshop dashboard from the project screenshot collection, showing an empty workshop and zero active records; no customer records are visible.
- **Forge**: verification captures of the Demo One organization. Its history and resulting measurements are generated demonstration data, as identified in the interface.

`images.json` records project-relative or descriptive source origins and provenance. Each `.webp.json` sidecar documents the authentic screenshot origin and conversion. Full-size WebP files preserve the original dimensions up to a maximum width of 1600 pixels; `-720.webp` files are proportional 720-pixel previews.
